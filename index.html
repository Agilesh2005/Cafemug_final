Our Journey

From a 30-seat rooftop café to a growing brand — here's how Cafemug has evolved, one cup at a time.

2019

The Beginning

Cafemug opened as Tiruppur's first rooftop café on Palladam Road — 30 seats and a 6,000 sq. ft. terrace garden. That same year, we launched the city's first fresh dough pizza shop.

6,000 sq. ft. terrace
2020

Growing Strong

Even through the lockdown, we partnered with leading online delivery platforms and crossed a major milestone in orders.

6,000+ online orders
2021

A Fresh Start

Through another lockdown, we used the time to reinvent — a renovated theme, a refreshed menu, and a brand-new celebration spot for special occasions.

2022

A Milestone Year

One of our proudest years — a record number of orders and guests came through our doors.

10,000+ orders · 9,000+ guests
2023

Expanding Our Family

We opened a new, fully air-conditioned dine-in outlet with 20 seats to welcome even more guests.

2024

Bigger Celebrations

With record dine-in visits, we moved our celebration spot to a larger, new location to host even more memorable moments.

12,000+ dine-in guests
2025

New Horizons

We expanded with a new branch on Kangeyam Road and took our first steps toward becoming a franchise brand.

2026

Brewing The Future

We invested in a professional coffee machine and crossed a major sales milestone — continuing our commitment to quality and growth.

₹50 lakhs+ in sales
